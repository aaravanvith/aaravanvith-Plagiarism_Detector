Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency, commonly known as TF-IDF, is a numerical statistic used to reflect how important a word is to a document in a collection of documents. TF-IDF converts text into numeric vectors that emphasize terms frequent in a document but rare across the corpus. This reduces the influence of common stopwords and boilerplate text that appear across many documents. Cosine similarity is often paired with TF-IDF vectors to measure the angle between two document vectors, with values ranging from zero to one, where higher values indicate stronger textual overlap.
