Academic integrity refers to the ethical code and moral values that govern research and scholarship. Plagiarism, defined as presenting someone else's work or ideas as one's own without proper attribution, undermines assessment fairness, learning outcomes, and institutional reputation. Universities increasingly rely on automated plagiarism detection systems to maintain academic integrity, as manual inspection of a large number of student submissions is time-consuming and prone to inconsistency. An effective detection system should be explainable, offering clear evidence rather than an opaque similarity score.
